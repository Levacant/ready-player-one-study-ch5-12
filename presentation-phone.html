Teacher questions
Answer before opening
What is imagery?
Show answer
Descriptive language/details that appeal to the senses and help the audience imagine an experience.
Does imagery need all five senses?
Show answer
No. It can use one or several senses.
Why is Ratatouille imagery?
Show answer
Its food scenes use sensory details like color, sound, texture, aroma, and flavor to make food vivid.
What senses are in your soup example?
Show answer
Smell, taste, and touch.
What is juxtaposition?
Show answer
Putting two things close together so differences or similarities stand out.
Why is Cinderella juxtaposition?
Show answer
Cinderella is contrasted with her stepsisters, making their different traits stand out.
Can something use both?
Show answer
Yes. Sensory details can make contrasting things vivid while juxtaposition emphasizes their difference.
What's the easiest difference?
Show answer
Imagery = sense it. Juxtaposition = compare it.
New examples
Decide: Imagery, Juxtaposition, Both, or Neither
The icy wind scratched my cheeks and made my fingers numb.
Reveal
Imagery
Touch details help us feel the cold.
A silent library is shown beside a screaming concert crowd.
Reveal
Juxtaposition
The contrasting settings are presented against each other.
Sweet cinnamon filled the warm kitchen while cookies crunched between my teeth.
Reveal
Imagery
Smell, touch, taste, and sound create a sensory experience.
A mansion is shown immediately after a tiny damaged apartment.
Reveal
Juxtaposition
The difference in living conditions is emphasized.
Two opposite settings are described with detailed sounds, smells, and textures.
Reveal
Both
Sensory details create imagery while the settings create juxtaposition.
A character walks into a room with no sensory detail or meaningful comparison.
Reveal
Neither
There is not enough sensory description or contrast for either technique.
Ready Player One — Imagery
Wade's real-world surroundings / the Stacks
The descriptions of Wade's crowded, deteriorating physical environment help readers picture his setting.
Why: concrete physical details build a sensory picture of his real-world life.
Ready Player One — Juxtaposition
Real world vs. OASIS
Wade's difficult physical world is placed against the expansive virtual OASIS.
Why: the contrast emphasizes why the OASIS feels like an escape and offers possibilities his real life does not.
