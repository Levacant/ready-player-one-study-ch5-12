Fast review

Big chain to remember

Halliday biography → Limerick → Ludus → Tomb of Horrors → Joust → Copper Key → Dungeons of Daggorath → First Gate → WarGames → next clue.

Clue chain

How the clues connect

  1. “Tomb filled with horrors” → Wade recognizes Tomb of Horrors.
  2. “Much to learn” → students → Ludus.
  3. Ludus also means school / sport / game in Latin.
  4. Skull-shaped hill on Ludus → entrance to the Tomb.
  5. Beat Acererak at Joust → earn the Copper Key.
  6. Key clue mentions Daggorath → Halliday's childhood room on Middletown.
  7. Beat Dungeons of Daggorath → First Gate opens.
  8. First Gate → reenact WarGames.
Quiz traps

Stuff that is easy to mix up

  • Halliday: born in Middletown, Ohio. Morrow: childhood friend/business partner.
  • Acererak = demi-lich in Tomb of Horrors. Anorak = Halliday's avatar.
  • Joust earns the Copper Key. Dungeons of Daggorath opens the First Gate.
  • WarGames is the First Gate movie; Wade plays David Lightman.
  • Wade reaches about Level 10 after the Copper Key and Level 20 after the First Gate.
  • Art3mis uses a Barrier spell after realizing Wade lied.
