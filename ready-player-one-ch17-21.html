CHAPTER 17

Art3mis & the distraction

Wade and Art3mis start exchanging private messages. Although she is hesitant, they become close, talk almost every day, go on OASIS dates, and kiss after The Rocky Horror Picture Show. Wade reaches level 99 but spends more time thinking about her than the Jade Key. He realizes the “captain” and “whistle” clue might refer to the Cap’n Crunch whistle, but still does not know where to search.

Remember: Wade's romance grows while progress on the Jade Key stalls.
Quick recall question

What cereal connects to the whistle clue?

Cap’n Crunch
CHAPTER 18

The birthday party attack

Ogden Morrow invites Wade to his 73rd birthday party at the Distracted Globe, a zero-gravity nightclub on planet Neonoir. Neonoir allows player-versus-player combat, so attending is risky. Wade arrives in a DeLorean and dances with Art3mis. When he tells her he loves her, she says he does not truly know her and that their relationship is distracting them from the Hunt. Armed Sixers attack the party. Ogden's avatar, Og, wipes them out with powerful red lightning. Art3mis leaves.

Remember: Og saves Wade and Art3mis from Sixers, but Wade loses his relationship.
Quick recall question

Who destroys the Sixer attackers?

Ogden Morrow / Og
CHAPTER 19

Wade's isolated life

After Art3mis cuts contact, Wade withdraws into his Columbus apartment. He upgrades his immersion equipment and uses a virtual assistant modeled on Max Headroom. He barely experiences the outside world. Recognizing the damage to his health, he enables fitness-lockout software that makes him exercise and follow a diet before he can log into the OASIS. His physical health improves, but he remains emotionally isolated.

Remember: The OASIS is Wade's escape, but his isolation is a serious problem.
Quick recall question

What forces Wade to exercise?

OASIS fitness-lockout software
CHAPTER 20

Six months without a breakthrough

Six months after the First Gate, Wade still has not found the Jade Key. He runs Parzival-TV, watches Art3mivision, and works an OASIS technical-support job to pay his bills. His friendship with Aech suffers after an argument. He completes a quest with Daito and Shoto, earning Hayata's Beta Capsule, which lets its holder transform into Ultraman for up to three minutes per day; he lets them keep it. At the end of a work shift, Wade discovers Art3mis has found the Jade Key.

Remember: Art3mis takes the lead in the search for the second key.
Quick recall question

What powerful artifact does Wade let Daito and Shoto keep?

Hayata's Beta Capsule
CHAPTER 21

The hunt for the Jade Key restarts

IOI has acquired Fyndoro's Tablet of Finding, an artifact that can reveal an avatar's location. After Art3mis finds the Jade Key, the Sixers use it to identify her location in Sector Seven, giving everyone a rough lead. Wade studies Sector Seven maps and identifies Archaide as a promising destination because it contains replicas of Halliday's Game Design of the Year awards. Art3mis's success renews Wade's determination to win.

Remember: Sector Seven and Archaide become the crucial leads.
Quick recall question

What sector does Art3mis find the Jade Key in?

Sector Seven
